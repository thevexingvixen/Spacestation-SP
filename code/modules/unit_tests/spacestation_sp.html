/datum/unit_test/sp_cook_a_dishThe chef's whole pipeline, from a pile of components to something worth serving.
/datum/unit_test/sp_curiosityWhat a nosy crew member will and will not walk off with.
/datum/unit_test/sp_triageWhat the medic decides once a patient has been looked over.

/datum/unit_test/sp_cryo_loadingPutting a beaker in a cryo tube and a patient after it, the way a doctor does: open it, drag them on, shut it.
/datum/unit_test/sp_cryo_freezerSetting the freezer by hand: alt-click steps its target temperature, ctrl-click turns it on.
/datum/unit_test/sp_surgery_planThe operation plan, worked out afresh from the patient's limbs each step: incise, repair, close, and then nothing.
/datum/unit_test/sp_surgery_hairlineA whole operation through the surgeon's own code: strapped to the table, the jumpsuit rolled out of the way, incision, repair and closure, then dressed and let up.
/datum/unit_test/sp_brew_plansThe chemist's plans, worked out from /tg/'s own reactions down to what the dispenser pours.
/datum/unit_test/sp_brewReal brews through the machines, stage by stage, with the amount and purity that come out of them. Libital needs the heater and sours as it forms; aiuri overheats five degrees past its best; cryoxadone takes four stages and must not meet its own ingredients in the wrong order.
/datum/unit_test/sp_chem_printPatches printed at the ChemMaster, the rest of the beaker poured away, and the patches picked up.
/datum/unit_test/sp_chem_bufferA heater with no buffer left gets some before anything else is brewed at it: each buffer brewed from the dispenser, drawn into the heater's store, and the rest of the batch poured away.
/datum/unit_test/sp_surgery_tray_reachA surgery tray in a corner, with a table on one side and the operating table on the other, is reached from the one diagonal tile left open, and that is where the doctor is sent to stand.
/datum/unit_test/sp_flaps_pathingThe pathfinder should not plan a standing crew member through plastic flaps they cannot walk through.
/datum/unit_test/sp_busy_ignores_chatterA doctor with a patient and a chemist with an order do not stop to answer chatter. The reply would pull them off the job, and a brew or an operation left running while the tree starts it again would run twice.
/datum/unit_test/sp_role_takeoverA job the AI crew fill is open to a joining player. A dead holder's place is freed without moving the body; a living holder goes off shift, off the manifest, bank account closed, and out of the round.
/datum/unit_test/sp_greytide_graffitiAn assistant draws one of the friendly designs on the floor with the crayon they carry.
/datum/unit_test/sp_greytide_bellAn assistant rings a desk bell, more than once.
/datum/unit_test/sp_greytide_lightsLights out in a room with somebody else in it, and back on again: the joke never leaves the room dark.
/datum/unit_test/sp_greytide_gearTool storage: budget insulated gloves go on, a crowbar goes in the bag, and then there is nothing more to want.
/datum/unit_test/sp_step_past_petsA crew member who walks into a pet that will not swap places steps round it. The HoS's giant spider, in combat mode like every basic mob, once kept them behind their own desk all shift.
/datum/unit_test/sp_armed_monkeysA monkey with a baton is only a monkey until it starts swinging it. A person holding one is still a worry.
/datum/unit_test/sp_behaviour_treesEvery SP controller has to point at a behaviour tree that was actually compiled.
/datum/unit_test/sp_pocket_from_floorCrew pick things up off the floor, but leave what somebody has set out on a table.
/datum/unit_test/sp_medic_restockA medic who has run out empties the nearest medical crate, and stops counting as low.
/datum/unit_test/sp_botany_requestBotany takes a request for aloe off the radio, and knows the aloe they carry is what was asked for.
/datum/unit_test/sp_rummage_takesRummaging takes things. A closet tips its contents onto the floor as it opens, and reading the contents afterwards found an empty box, so crew opened lockers all shift and took nothing.
/datum/unit_test/sp_restock_asks_onceMedics do not ask twice: a crate already on order, or aloe botany is already after, keeps them quiet.
/datum/unit_test/sp_crate_matches_orderA crate is matched to its request on the order number the shuttle stamps on it, not on the pack's name: medbay's Medical Supplies Crate arrives as a DeForest Medical crate, and sat unclaimed in the cargo bay.
/datum/unit_test/sp_crate_claimsOne technician per crate. Two who picked the same one took the pull off each other across the station and both gave up, so a crate somebody is already on their way to is left to them.
/datum/unit_test/sp_drop_spot_short_of_doorA delivery for a room the courier cannot get into is left short of the first door they cannot open, rather than walked at that door until the haul times out. With access to the door, it goes all the way.
/datum/unit_test/sp_rummage_relocksWhoever unlocks a locker to rummage in it locks it again, rather than leaving a head's locker open to anybody.
/datum/unit_test/sp_medic_finds_deliveryA crate cargo delivered for medbay is found wherever the technician managed to leave it, in sight or not.
/datum/unit_test/sp_botany_cooks_requestBotany cooks the aloe medbay asked for into the burn cream they wanted, in a real microwave.
/datum/unit_test/sp_medic_takes_left_suppliesSupplies left for a medic by name are found wherever they were put down, and picked up.
/datum/unit_test/sp_request_names_destinationA request that says where to send it goes there, and medbay's asks go to medbay, not wherever the medic stood.
/datum/unit_test/sp_patient_shown_outA patient let go inside medbay is buzzed back out through its doors for a while, and through nobody else's.
/datum/unit_test/sp_requested_seed_firstA seed somebody asked for goes into the next free tray ahead of routine work, unless it is already growing.
/datum/unit_test/sp_scheme_stealA steal scheme reads incomplete until the pawn is carrying the target, and complete once they are.
/datum/unit_test/sp_crime_witnessesWho counts as a witness to a crime, and what a callout does: assistants and schemers keep quiet, an ordinary crew member calls it out, thinks less of the culprit, and (with a headset) files a suspect.
/datum/unit_test/sp_break_lightBreaking a light tube leaves it dark. The malicious greytide's one built behaviour, at the point it acts.
/datum/unit_test/sp_steal_target_liftableA steal target has to be something a thief could actually walk up to and lift.
/datum/unit_test/sp_steal_target_reachableA thief only takes on a target they can actually walk to with their own ID.
/datum/unit_test/sp_security_confronts_suspectA crime reported with a suspect and no attacker sends security to have a word, not to swing a baton.
/datum/unit_test/sp_crime_record_escalatesCrimes go on the record, and a pattern on the record is what turns the next word into an arrest.
/datum/unit_test/sp_order_relayAn order from the head of security reaches whoever hears it, and changes only what it names.
/datum/unit_test/sp_arming_finds_nested_batonThe whole point of fetching a belt: the baton is inside it, and a deep search is what finds it.
/datum/unit_test/sp_sentence_escalatesA sentence is measured off the record, and never longer than the door timer would actually hold.
/datum/unit_test/sp_cell_doorway_faces_the_lockerA cell's inside is the side of its door's edge that the cell's locker stands on, whatever plain distance says.
/datum/unit_test/sp_closed_closet_is_emptyA closed closet is empty until somebody opens it, which is why the locker search cannot trust its contents.
/proc/sp_test_owes_answerWhether a crew member owes someone an answer, or a look up.
/proc/sp_test_answeringWho a crew member is answering: somebody owed a line, somebody they looked up at, or their partner in a thread.
/proc/sp_test_hushLets a crew member be spoken to afresh. A test runs inside one tick, where "this same line" (free_to_talk()) would otherwise cover every line said in it.
/proc/sp_test_treeA compiled behaviour tree, as nested lists.
/proc/sp_test_tree_hasWhether a node of type wanted can be reached in a compiled tree, following subtrees into their own trees, along a path through no node of type not_under.
/datum/unit_test/sp_trees_talkEvery crew member can be answered, looks up at their name and starts chats, and a newcomer's hello never waits on the chat cooldown. Base crew had no chat at all, security answered nobody, and greetings shared a cooldown with failed partner searches.
/datum/unit_test/sp_speech_whole_wordsSpeech is read in whole words: "hey" is not in "they", "Tom" is not in "tomorrow", and a bare "hi" is a greeting.
/datum/unit_test/sp_distress_needs_urgencyA player's call for help is urgent or violent, and a calm request is neither.
/datum/unit_test/sp_answer_onceStanding moves once for each line answered, a name on its own gets a look up, and a late answer is dropped.
/datum/unit_test/sp_crew_exchangeTwo crew hold a written conversation end to end: it starts, runs line by line, and lets both of them go.
/datum/unit_test/sp_one_answer_per_lineA line naming nobody gets one answer, from the nearest crew member free to give it; a line naming someone else is left to them.
/datum/unit_test/sp_security_answersOfficers answer people when they are free, and not in the middle of an arrest.
/datum/unit_test/sp_cell_swap_puts_them_inThe one move that puts a prisoner in a cell: the officer swaps out past them, or drags them the last step when they are lying down and there is nothing to swap with. An escort under way also holds its branch against a fresh report, which used to swap the target out from under the officer mid-walk.
/datum/unit_test/sp_baton_switched_onA baton comes out of the belt switched off, and an inactive one is a club: the hit falls through to brute.
/datum/unit_test/sp_petty_arrest_stays_nonlethalLethal force is for threats, not for a smashed light tube: a petty arrest stays on stun at any alert level.
/datum/unit_test/sp_arm_locker_usableSomewhere to draw a kit from has to be somewhere this officer can actually open.
/datum/unit_test/sp_order_acted_on_onceAn order is acted on once per listener. Before this, every word from the head of security for five seconds after an order was swallowed as that order, attacks they reported included.
/datum/unit_test/sp_janitor_finds_a_mopA mop lying on a floor is one to pick up; a mop somebody else is holding is not.
/datum/unit_test/sp_janitor_keeps_the_mop_wetA dry mop cleans nothing, so it is filled first: at a bucket, a cart or a sink.
/datum/unit_test/sp_janitor_leaves_maintenance_for_lastThe nearest mess wins, except in maintenance, which waits: the crew see the hallways.
/datum/unit_test/sp_janitor_mops_it_upMopping actually removes the mess, through TG's own cleaning.
/datum/unit_test/sp_janitor_picks_up_a_peelA banana peel is picked up rather than mopped, which is what the clown leaves behind.
/datum/unit_test/sp_clown_peels_in_publicA peel goes down in the hallways, not in medbay: somebody going over on the way to a patient is not a joke.
/datum/unit_test/sp_clown_drops_a_peelThe banana becomes a peel on the floor, which is the whole point of the banana.
/datum/unit_test/sp_clown_honks_at_peopleHonking wants an audience, and takes the horn out of the clown's pocket to do it.
/datum/unit_test/sp_clown_asks_botanyOut of bananas, the clown asks botany, in words botany actually listens for.
/datum/unit_test/sp_dialogue_files_are_soundEvery dialogue we ship loads and hangs together, and a broken one is refused rather than half-run.
/datum/unit_test/sp_dialogue_casts_by_jobRoles are cast by job, whichever of the two walked over.
/datum/unit_test/sp_dialogue_thread_runsA thread runs line by line to an end, moves standing on the way, and is not had twice in a row.
/datum/unit_test/sp_arrest_is_not_an_attackA person being arrested shouting that the officer attacked them is not a crime report.
/datum/unit_test/sp_janitor_leaves_shut_rooms_aloneA room the janitor cannot get into is left alone as a room, not one stain at a time.

/datum/unit_test/sp_dialogue_conditionsEvery condition a file can ask about answers both ways.
/datum/unit_test/sp_dialogue_effectsEvery effect a file can have does what it says, and a gift is only ever something the giver carries.
/datum/unit_test/sp_dialogue_validationThe loader refuses what it cannot run, and says why.
/datum/unit_test/sp_player_repliesA player's turn: replies on offer, nobody else can take them, and silence goes where the file says.
/datum/unit_test/sp_player_speech_opens_dialogueA stranger saying hello is introduced; somebody they know gets a hello back; saying your name teaches it.
/datum/unit_test/sp_id_read_up_closeA name is read off an ID from beside somebody, and not from across a room.
/datum/unit_test/sp_standing_on_examineHow a crew member regards you shows on examine at the extremes, and not in between.
/datum/unit_test/sp_talk_offers_what_fitsThe Talk to list offers what fits the two of you now, and nothing that does not.
/datum/unit_test/sp_topics_are_filesThe seven keyword topics are dialogue files now, alongside the rest.
/datum/unit_test/sp_reply_link_round_tripA reply link leads back to its conversation, and a click only counts from the player it was offered to: the whole of what happens server-side when a player clicks one, from the link's own text.
/datum/unit_test/sp_busy_crew_say_soSomebody busy with work says so when a player names them, and carries on with the job.
/datum/unit_test/sp_no_conversation_out_of_reachA conversation does not start between two people too far apart to keep it going, and a player calling out from over there is answered with a line instead. Found by the stand-in: a hello heard from seven tiles started an introduction that died on its first line, and the player got silence.
/datum/unit_test/sp_one_conversation_at_a_timeOne conversation at a time for a player: nobody starts a second introduction with somebody already talking, but a player who turns to somebody by name is taken up whatever else is pending.
/datum/unit_test/sp_player_outranks_small_talkA player outranks small talk. Talk to still offers what fits while somebody chats with a colleague, and picking from it -- or naming them -- breaks the chat off. The stand-in's fifth visit lost both of the people it went to see to colleagues within seconds, and Talk to came up empty both times.
/datum/unit_test/sp_no_small_talk_while_engagedSomebody a player has just been talking to does not wander off into small talk while the player is still there, and is not drawn into any; once the player has gone, they are free again.
/datum/unit_test/sp_introduction_counts_as_greetingBeing introduced counts as being greeted, however it started: nobody tells somebody they have just met that they didn't see them come in.
/datum/unit_test/sp_mimes_keep_quietA mime is cast in no spoken part and drawn into no small talk, and waves at a newcomer rather than greeting them in words TG will not let them say.
/proc/sp_test_give_idDresses somebody in a jumpsuit with an ID clipped to it, carrying these accesses.
/proc/sp_test_run_threadPlays a thread through to its end, a line at a time, all within the test's tick.
/datum/unit_test/sp_favours_are_earnedA favour is offered only where it can be done, granted only to somebody liked well enough, and costs a point of that standing when it is.
/datum/unit_test/sp_favour_followSomebody who agreed to come along sets off after you, and is let off with a thank you, or when the time is up.
/datum/unit_test/sp_favour_doorA door is opened for somebody who cannot open it, by somebody whose own card can, and held until they are through; never one to anywhere nobody opens for anybody else.
/datum/unit_test/sp_favour_fetchFetching: the nearest one lying about in their own department, picked up, brought back and put in our hands.
/datum/unit_test/sp_favour_calls_a_doctorCalling medbay: the call goes out on the radio, one medic takes it, and comes to that person wherever they are.
/proc/sp_test_give_jobGives a test mob a job, for dialogue cast by job and department.
/datum/unit_test/sp_work_opens_conversationsWhat the crew do together opens the conversation written for it -- a drink served, a patient seen to, produce dropped off in the kitchen -- and the second time round is not the first.
/datum/unit_test/sp_botany_requests_wait_their_turnA botanist already growing something for somebody takes no other request, except medbay's aloe, which comes first.

/datum/unit_test/sp_loot_is_recognisedWhat an officer takes off somebody: the thing they were seen taking, contraband, a weapon on a civilian, and what belongs to another job; never the ID.
/datum/unit_test/sp_search_takes_the_lootThe pat-down after an arrest takes the loot off them and into the officer's bag, and nothing else.
/datum/unit_test/sp_escort_takes_hold_againThe escort takes hold of the prisoner again after losing them on the way, and lets go after too many lost holds.
/datum/unit_test/sp_thief_is_asked_firstA thief is asked for it first: crew with nothing to hide hand it over, and a schemer is searched for it.
/datum/unit_test/sp_evidence_finds_its_homeWhere evidence goes: the evidence closet for whoever can open it, the warden or the head of security for an officer who cannot, and a security locker once a keeper has proved impossible to reach or there is none.
/datum/unit_test/sp_evidence_is_filedFiling: into the closet, which is locked again behind it.
/datum/unit_test/sp_warden_lets_them_outThe warden lets a prisoner out when their time is up, and shuts the cell again once they have gone.
/datum/unit_test/sp_warden_calls_an_escapeA prisoner out of the cell with time left is put back on the arrest list, and the cell freed for the next one.
/datum/unit_test/sp_warden_minds_the_brigThe warden minds the brig: a report from elsewhere on the station is somebody else's, one from the brig is theirs.
/datum/unit_test/sp_warden_locks_upAn armoury locker left unlocked on a quiet shift is locked up again.
/datum/unit_test/sp_security_remembers_the_pranksterSecurity have a word with the assistant who pulled a prank, and only with that one, and keep an eye on them after.
/datum/unit_test/sp_all_access_is_refusedThe Head of Personnel turns down all access, remembers who asked, and has less patience the second time.
