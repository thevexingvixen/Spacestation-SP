/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

code/modules/spacestation_sp/sp_body.dm

/proc/sp_is_bodyA dead crew member lying somewhere, as opposed to one on a morgue tray, in a bag, or carried; monkeys are not crew.
/proc/sp_body_unreportedWhether this body wants calling out: not yet, or so long ago that it is plainly still lying there.
/proc/sp_notice_bodyA crew member has a body in sight. A medic takes it on themselves and the detective takes the scene; anybody says so, and with a headset reports it (sp_body_seen()). Called every second by whoever can see it, so it has to be cheap when there is nothing new to do.
/proc/sp_body_seenA crew member has found a body. They say so, and with a headset report it over the radio as an incident with the dead in it and nobody named, which medbay takes as a call to come and security as a scene. Returns TRUE if it was reported; a body already called out is left to whoever is on their way.
/proc/sp_crew_by_nameA crew member by their real name, or null.
/proc/sp_medic_defibThe defibrillator a medic carries, charged enough for a shock, or null.
/proc/sp_defib_verdictWhat TG's verdict on a body means, for the log.
/proc/sp_try_reviveOne go with the paddles, the way a player does it: the paddles out of the unit, wielded in both hands, and placed on the chest, which TG takes five seconds over. Only when TG itself says the body can be revived (can_defib()); on any other body the paddles would only buzz, so the reason is logged and remembered instead. A body too far gone wants repairing first, which is cryo or surgery on a corpse and is not written; it goes to the morgue (docs/03 §3). Returns TRUE if the body came back. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_put_paddles_awayPuts the paddles back on the unit and the unit away.
/proc/sp_find_morgueThe nearest morgue tray on this level with nobody in it and nothing locking it, or null.
/proc/sp_morgue_tray_turfThe tile the tray slides out onto.
/proc/sp_morgue_spotAn open tile to stand on beside the tray and within reach of the slab, nearest to near: never the tray's own tile, which would take the medic in with the body, and never a tile in front of the tray, from which the slab cannot be clicked (the first medic to reach the morgue stood there and could not get the tray out).
/proc/sp_store_in_morguePuts a body on the morgue tray and shuts it: the slab opened, the body dragged onto the tray the way a player does it, and the slab shut again. The medic stands beside the tray, never on it. Returns TRUE once the body is inside. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_prints_onThe prints on something, as a plain list of print strings.
/proc/sp_whose_printsCrew records whose fingerprint is among these prints, as names: a print is the md5 of a DNA identity, which the manifest keeps.
/proc/sp_read_sceneWhat a scene says. The dead first: whoever gripped or hit them with their hands left prints on what they wear. Then whatever is lying within a few tiles, a dropped weapon above all, which carries the prints of whoever swung it. The dead's own prints and the detective's are not evidence. Returns suspect name -> the things their prints were on.
/proc/sp_investigate_sceneThe detective looks the scene over: the scanner on the body, for everyone to see, and then the reading above. A name found goes on the arrest list and the record, and to security over the radio as an attack with a known attacker, which is the baton path. Returns the suspect, or null. Sleeps.
