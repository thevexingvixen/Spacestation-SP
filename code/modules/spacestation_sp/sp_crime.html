code/modules/spacestation_sp/sp_crime.dm

/proc/sp_witnessesEveryone awake who could see culprit right now, AI crew and players alike, other than the culprit.
/proc/sp_crime_witnessesThe witnesses who would do something about it: any player, since a player might, and AI crew who report crimes at all. Assistants do not grass on each other, and anyone with a scheme of their own keeps their head down.
/proc/sp_crime_seenSomebody might have seen that. Each AI crew member who would tell gets a chance to notice, in no particular order, and the first who does calls it out (sp_call_out_crime()). Players see it for themselves. Returns whoever noticed, or null.
/proc/sp_call_out_crimeA witness calls out what they saw and holds it against the culprit; with a headset, they also tell security where. The report names a suspect but no attacker, so officers who hear it walk over and look rather than reach for a baton.
/proc/sp_file_crime_recordWrites a witnessed crime onto the suspect's security record, the way an officer would at the console, and hands back how many crimes they now have against their name (0 if they have no record at all — a visitor, or anyone the manifest never learned about).
/proc/sp_mark_for_arrestPuts somebody on the arrest list, so an officer meeting them later already knows, and the secbots too.
/proc/sp_sentence_timeHow long the cell door stays shut, measured by what is already on their record.
/proc/sp_free_cellThe nearest cell nobody is already serving time in. A timer that is running has an occupant behind it.
/proc/sp_cell_doorwayThe two tiles either side of a cell's door: just inside, and just outside.
/proc/sp_cell_tile_freeSomewhere a person can stand: not a wall, and nothing dense on it other than a door or another person.
/proc/sp_wanted_for_arrestWhether this person's record says they are to be taken in.
/proc/sp_is_arrestingWhether officer is in the middle of arresting who.
