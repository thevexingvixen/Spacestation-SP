code/modules/spacestation_sp/sp_curiosity.dm

/proc/sp_roaming_areasSpacestation SP — idle curiosity.
/proc/sp_roll_interestsRolls this character's tastes.
/proc/sp_find_rummage_targetA locker, crate or box worth opening: shut, not welded or broken, and either unlocked or something our ID opens. Deliberately oview: this is idle nosiness, so it only covers what we can actually see from where we are standing, not the whole department.
/proc/sp_worth_a_lookCould we open this, and is there any point?
/proc/sp_tempting_contentsEverything in the container this person would actually take.
/proc/sp_rummage_containerOpens a container, takes what this person fancies out of it, and shuts it again unless they are the sort to leave it hanging open. Returns the names of what was taken. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_relockLocks a closet again if we were the ones who unlocked it. A research director who took their headset out and left the locker unlocked behind them left it for the next scientist to help themselves, and one did. Sleeps, like any click.
/proc/sp_find_loose_itemSomething worth picking up off the floor. Only the floor: anything on a table or a rack was put there by somebody — the chef's ingredients, the chemist's patches, a beaker of cryoxadone waiting for a tube — and helping yourself to that is theft rather than magpie curiosity.
/proc/sp_pocket_loose_itemPicks something up off the floor: into the bag if it fits, otherwise a hand. TRUE if we have it.
/proc/sp_find_door_to_tryA door we cannot open. The point is the rebuff, so this deliberately looks for one that will refuse us — an ordinary crew member finds out the hard way that engineering is not for them.
