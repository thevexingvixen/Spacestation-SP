/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

code/modules/spacestation_sp/sp_chemistry.dm

/proc/sp_chem_areasSpacestation SP — chemistry.
/proc/sp_in_chem_labWhether something is in the chemistry lab, the pharmacy or chem storage.
/proc/sp_is_chem_dispenserThe plain chemistry dispenser, as opposed to the bar's taps and the debug synthesizers that share its type.
/proc/sp_reagent_purityHow pure one reagent in a container is, or 0 if it is not there.
/datum/sp_brew_stageOne stage of a brew: what goes in from the dispenser, and the reaction that should follow.
/datum/sp_brewA medicine planned from the dispenser up: the stages in the order they have to happen.
/proc/sp_reactions_makingThe reactions that make this reagent which a chemistry dispenser could feed: a single result, no special container, no catalyst to find, and not one that only goes when frozen.
/proc/sp_reaction_rateUnits a second a reaction makes at this temperature, by /tg/'s own curve.
/proc/sp_brew_temperatureHow warm a stage should be held. Room temperature when the reaction goes happily there; otherwise as close to its best as it can get with a margin under the point where it overheats. Aiuri's best is 300 and it overheats — flash, bang — at 315, so that margin is not a formality.
/proc/sp_plan_stagesPlans the stages that make want units of product, deepest first, onto the end of stages. Returns FALSE if some part of it cannot be got to from what the dispenser pours.
/proc/sp_brew_conflictThe first reaction, other than the one a stage is for, that the beaker's contents would satisfy at some point in the brew, or null if there is none. Everything goes into one beaker and the reagent system fires whatever it can, so a stage that also satisfies some other reaction might make a different medicine — or smoke powder, or thermite; the dispenser pours the makings of both.
/proc/sp_plan_brewA way to make a medicine from what the dispenser pours, in the biggest batch that fits the beaker and comes out in whole units at every addition — the dispenser pours whole units, so a plan wanting a third of a unit of carbon is not one a chemist could follow. Null when it cannot be made, or when some stage would give another reaction the chance to go instead.
/proc/sp_brew_whole_atWhether every addition comes out a whole number of units at this scale.
/proc/sp_explain_brewWhy a medicine could not be planned, for a test failure or a log line.
/proc/sp_chem_insertClicks a beaker we carry into a machine. TRUE if it went in. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_chem_ejectTakes a beaker back out of a machine: a right-click with a free hand. TRUE if we have it again. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_buffer_phKeeps a reacting beaker's pH where its reaction wants it, a unit of the heater's buffer at a time, the way a chemist leans on the heater's buffer buttons. Libital sours by six and a half pH as it forms; left alone a batch ends in the acid, and every step made there comes out less pure than the last.
/proc/sp_brew_ph_driftHow far a stage's reaction moves the pH by the time it is done: the whole H_ion_release for one that /tg/ scales to the batch, otherwise that much for every unit made.
/proc/sp_brew_start_phWhere a stage's pH should start so its own drift carries it through the good range rather than out of it: low for aiuri, which turns basic as it forms, high for libital, which turns acid.
/proc/sp_brew_needs_bufferWhether a stage starting at this pH would start, or drift, out of its reaction's good range.
/proc/sp_prebufferSets a stage's pH before its last ingredient goes in, so the reaction starts where it should rather than where its ingredients happen to land. Aiuri is done in about a second and a half, so buffering once it has started is too late: a batch that starts at pH 3.6 comes out barely a third pure. Needs the beaker in the heater, whose buffers these are.
/proc/sp_chem_buffersThe heater's two buffers, the one the brews use most first: libital, aiuri and cryoxadone all want the basic.
/proc/sp_chem_buffer_wantedThe buffer a heater is running short of, if the dispenser can make it. A heater starts with twenty units of each, which is three brews' worth, and the first live chemist found out what the brew after that is like.
/proc/sp_load_bufferPuts a batch of buffer where the brews can use it: into the heater's store, up to the half of the heater it keeps for each, and whatever is left down the ChemMaster. Returns TRUE if the heater took any. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_react_stageHolds the beaker in the heater at the stage's temperature until its reaction has made what it should, or has stopped. Returns how much of the stage's product there is. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_run_brewBrews a planned medicine at a bench: each stage's additions from the dispenser, then its reaction in the heater. Returns how many units of the medicine came out. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_chem_drainPours a beaker away down the ChemMaster: into its buffer, and the buffer emptied. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_print_patchesTurns the medicine in a beaker into patches at the ChemMaster: in goes the beaker, the medicine into the buffer, out come the patches onto the machine's tile, and whatever else was in the beaker goes down the drain. Returns the patches, picked up. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_chem_find_beakerThe smallest empty beaker we carry. A standard beaker's batch is a few patches or a tube's worth of cryoxadone, and a bigger batch needs more of the heater's buffer than it holds: twenty units of each, and a 27u batch of libital takes about eight. Null if nothing we carry is clean.
/proc/sp_make_medicineMakes one order at a bench: finds a clean beaker, plans the brew for it, brews it, and prints patches if that is the form it is wanted in. Returns TRUE if there is medicine to deliver. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_find_chem_benchA place to stand with a chemistry dispenser, a heater and a ChemMaster all in reach, so a whole brew happens without walking: list(turf, dispenser, heater, chem master), the nearest. MetaStation builds all three of its benches this way, with a chemist's spawn point on the standing tile.
/proc/sp_chem_fridgesThe chemistry fridges in the lab, which medbay reaches into through the wall.
/proc/sp_find_chem_fridgeThe nearest chemistry fridge with room in it.
/proc/sp_chem_patch_stockPatches of a medicine waiting in the chemistry fridges.
/proc/sp_carried_patchesPatches of a medicine the chemist is carrying, which will be in the fridge as soon as they have walked over.
/proc/sp_cryoxadone_supplyCryoxadone for the tubes, in units: what is in them, and what is waiting in the cryo room.
/proc/sp_find_cryo_dropWhere fresh cryoxadone goes: the table in the cryo room nearest the tubes.
/proc/sp_next_chem_orderWhat the chemist should make next at a bench (sp_find_chem_bench's list), as list(product, form), or null when medbay has what it needs. Buffer for the bench's heater comes first, since every brew after it leans on it; then cryo, because a patient in a tube with nothing in the beaker is only getting cold; then patches.
/proc/sp_next_chem_dropWhere the medicine we carry should go, as list(target, item): cryoxadone to cryo, patches to the fridge.
