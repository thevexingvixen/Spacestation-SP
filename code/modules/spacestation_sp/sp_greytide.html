/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

code/modules/spacestation_sp/sp_greytide.dm

/proc/sp_greytide_hauntsSpacestation SP — greytide, gently.
/proc/sp_greytide_interestsAn assistant's tastes: gloves, masks, toys and one tool, on top of whatever they roll like anyone else.
/proc/sp_is_graffiti_areaFloor an assistant draws on: hallways, maintenance and the commons, nobody's workplace.
/proc/sp_is_prank_roomRooms where the lights going out for a few seconds is a joke rather than a problem.
/proc/sp_is_off_limitsSomewhere an assistant has no business, whose windows are therefore worth knocking on.
/proc/sp_is_public_spotSomewhere anyone may stand: the public areas, and the lobbies in front of the departments.
/proc/sp_security_watchingWhether anyone from security is watching: no assistant pulls a prank in front of an officer.
/proc/sp_people_in_areaHow many other people, awake and on their feet, are in a room.
/proc/sp_public_reach_spotA tile beside something on its public side, to stand on and reach it from: a counter has two sides.
/proc/sp_carried_crayonA crayon we carry. Not a spray can: those paint faces as readily as floors.
/proc/sp_find_graffiti_spotSomewhere to draw: a bit of public floor close by, clear and not already drawn on.
/proc/sp_find_light_prankA light switch in a room with people in it and the lights on, where the dark is only a joke.
/proc/sp_find_light_to_breakA working light in a public area worth smashing, with a tile to reach its fixture from. The malicious end of the menu: it stays dark until a crew member replaces the tube, so only where that is a nuisance rather than a hazard — the same public rooms the harmless lights-out prank uses, never a workplace.
/proc/sp_find_window_to_knockA window into somewhere off limits, with public floor outside it to knock from.
/proc/sp_find_bell_to_ringA desk bell nearby, on a counter whose public side we can stand at.
/proc/sp_find_honkA horn, or a rubber duck, that we carry, and somebody about to hear it.
/proc/sp_mischief_optionsEvery prank open to this assistant where they stand, as list(kind, target, tile to do it from) entries.
/proc/sp_prank_vandalismSmashes a light tube. Goes dark until somebody replaces it, and a witness calls it out. TRUE if it broke. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_prank_graffitiDraws on a bit of floor beside or under us with a crayon we carry. TRUE if a drawing appeared. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_prank_lightsLights out in a room with people in it, a few seconds of dark, and the lights back on. They always come back on, whatever happens in between: a joke that leaves the bar dark all shift is not one. TRUE if the room went dark. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_prank_knockKnocks on a window into somewhere off limits and asks to be let in. TRUE if they knocked. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_prank_bellRings a desk bell a few times and asks for service. TRUE if it rang. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_prank_honkHonks a horn, or squeaks a duck, at whoever is about. TRUE if it made a noise. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_gear_wantedWhat an assistant still wants from tool storage, as lists of types that would do: gloves first, then a tool.
/proc/sp_find_gearThe nearest loose item of these sorts in tool storage, and the tile to reach it from, as list(item, spot), or null when tool storage has nothing left that will do.
/proc/sp_take_gear_itemTakes an item from tool storage: gloves go on, anything else goes in the bag. TRUE if we have it.
