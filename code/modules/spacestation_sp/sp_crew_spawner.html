code/modules/spacestation_sp/sp_crew_spawner.dm

/proc/sp_spawn_crew_memberClient-less crew spawning.
/proc/sp_send_to_postPuts a new crew member at their post if the job's own spawn point dropped them at arrivals.
/proc/sp_controller_for_jobPicks the AI controller type for a job based on its department.
/proc/sp_essential_job_typesJobs the station cannot do without; populate fills one of each before anything else (after heads). Botany and the kitchen are here because they feed each other: a station with nobody growing anything is a station where the chef has nothing to cook, and a station with no chef is one where the crew eat out of a vending machine all shift.
/proc/sp_get_crew_job_poolReturns the list of jobs an AI crew member may be spawned as: joinable, human, station crew.
/proc/sp_populate_stationSpawns count AI crew spread across the job pool, respecting each job's spawn_positions. Heads of staff are filled first so the station has a command structure, then the rest round-robin. Returns the number actually spawned.
/proc/sp_spawn_assistantsSpawns count AI assistants, the station's greytide. Returns how many made it.
