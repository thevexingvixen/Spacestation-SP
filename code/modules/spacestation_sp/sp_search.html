/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

code/modules/spacestation_sp/sp_search.dm

/proc/sp_is_lootSearches and confiscation: what security take off somebody, and where it goes.
/proc/sp_is_somebody_elsesWhether an item is on the steal catalogue and this is not one of the jobs it belongs to. The catalogue is what antagonists steal from (sp_antagonist.dm), and what it lists is by definition somebody's: the captain keeps their medal, and a cook found with it has some explaining to do.
/proc/sp_loot_onEverything on somebody an officer would take: worn, in their hands, and in their bag.
/proc/sp_take_from_personTakes one thing off somebody and into the officer's keeping: their bag, their hands, or at their feet.
/proc/sp_hold_evidenceAdds to what we are carrying as evidence. A list value has to go in with override_blackboard_key.
/proc/sp_evidence_carriedWhat we are carrying as evidence that is still on us.
/proc/sp_search_personPats somebody down and takes what they should not have. Sleeps for the search itself. Returns what was taken, which is now the officer's to file (BB_SP_EVIDENCE).
/proc/sp_hand_over_lootA thief handing over what they were asked for, rather than being searched for it. Returns what was given.
/proc/sp_hands_overWhether somebody told to hand something over will: crew with nothing to hide do, and a schemer never does.
/proc/sp_item_names"a baton and a medal", from a list of items.
/proc/sp_nearest_openable_closetThe nearest closet of closet_type on our level that we could open (sp_arm_locker_usable()).
/proc/sp_evidence_keeperSomebody on duty who can open the evidence closet and is not us: the warden first, then the head of security.
/proc/sp_evidence_homeWhere what we are carrying as evidence goes: the evidence closet if our card opens it; otherwise whoever's does, to hand it to; otherwise -- or once the walk to them has come to nothing too often -- a security locker we can open.
