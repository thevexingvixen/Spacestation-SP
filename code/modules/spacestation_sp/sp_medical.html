/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

code/modules/spacestation_sp/sp_medical.dm

/proc/sp_in_medbayWhether someone standing here has come to medbay: the lobby, the treatment floor, cryo, surgery, the wards.
/proc/sp_medbay_delivery_areaWhere a delivery for medbay should go: the lobby, which anybody bringing something over can walk into, else the treatment floor. Medics notice they are out wherever they happen to be, and in the first round with them roaming one asked cargo from the fitness room and the other asked botany from the cargo warehouse, which is where the crate and the aloe were then sent. Returns the lobby's type even on a map without one, so it reads the same in a test.
/proc/sp_patch_damageBrute plus burn: the damage a medkit, a patch or the cryo tubes can do something about.
/proc/sp_needs_checkupWhether this crew member has something worth walking to medbay about.
/proc/sp_medic_on_dutyWhether anyone in medical, AI or player, is on their feet on this z-level to see a patient.
/proc/sp_hold_stillAsks an AI patient to stay where they are while a medic works on them: a patient who wanders off mid-stitch breaks the do_after and the medic has to start again. Players are left to their own judgement.
/proc/sp_release_patientLets a patient go again, if it was us holding them, and sees them out of medbay if they are inside it.
/proc/sp_see_outLets a patient back out of medbay. Medics drag the badly hurt through doors only staff can open, into the cryo tubes and onto the operating table, and a quartermaster who came out of cryo at full health spent the rest of the round standing in the cryo room, unable to walk back to their console, while cargo's orders went unplaced. For a few minutes their route may go through medbay's doors, and a medbay door they walk into opens for them, as if the desk had buzzed them through (sp_buzz_through()).
/proc/sp_buzz_throughA medbay door opening for a patient on their way out, the way the desk buzzes people through. Only medbay's own doors, only while they are being seen out, and never one that is bolted or has no power. Returns TRUE if it opens.
/proc/sp_medic_supply_countUses of treatment a medic is carrying: every charge on every medical stack, plus one for each patch.
/proc/sp_medic_low_on_suppliesWhether a medic is low enough on treatment to go and find more of it.
/proc/sp_counts_as_suppliesWhether a medic would restock with this: a medical stack, a patch, or a whole medkit.
/proc/sp_supplies_insideWhat in a locker or crate a medic would restock with.
/proc/sp_find_supply_storeSomewhere to restock from: the nearest locker or crate in sight with treatment supplies in it that we could open, else supplies somebody left for us by name, else a crate cargo delivered for medbay.
/proc/sp_restock_fromEmpties what a medic needs out of a locker or crate: stacks, patches and medkits, up to a few. Returns how many things were taken. Sleeps: a locked closet takes one click to unlock and another to open.
/proc/sp_pick_up_left_suppliesPicks up supplies that were left out for us, botany's cream on a lobby table say. Returns 1 if we have them.
/proc/sp_needs_medicWhether a patient needs a medic at all: hurt enough to matter, bleeding, or down.
/proc/sp_triageWhat to do with a patient once they have been looked over. Serious cases go in the cryo tube when one is ready. A surgeon operates on broken and dislocated bones, and on serious cases when no tube is ready. Everybody else gets whatever the medkit has.
/proc/sp_limb_urgencyHow badly a limb wants seeing to: its damage, with bleeding counted heavily.
/proc/sp_limbs_by_damageThe patient's limbs, worst first. Built fresh, because sorting the real list would reorder their body.
/proc/sp_describe_injuriesWhat a medic says reading a scan back to the patient: "25 brute, mostly your left arm".
/proc/sp_medic_equippedWhether this medic carries anything to look a patient over or treat them with.
/proc/sp_patient_takenWhether another AI medic already has this patient.
/proc/sp_find_patientWho to see next: anybody hurt who has come into medbay, anybody hurt within sight of wherever we are, and any player standing in medbay we have not scanned lately, so a player walking in gets looked at like anyone else. Somebody down comes before everybody standing; then the worst hurt; then the nearest.
/proc/sp_give_up_on_patientStops working on a patient for a while: we could not get to them, or nothing we had would help.
/proc/sp_stack_can_treatWhether a medkit stack would do anything for that part of the patient, as the stack itself judges it: damage it heals, a burn it dresses, bleeding it stops, and skin it can get at through their clothes.
/proc/sp_patch_can_treatWhether a patch is worth putting on that limb: it treats what the limb has, and none is on there already.
/proc/sp_pick_treatmentThe best thing we carry for the worst limb we can do anything about, as list(item, zone), or null. Stacks first, since they work on the spot; patches for whatever the stacks cannot reach.
/proc/sp_take_in_handPuts one particular item we carry into our active hand, making room if we must. TRUE if it is there.
/proc/sp_treat_roundOne round of treatment: aims at the worst limb we can do something for, puts the right thing in hand and applies it. Sutures and mesh carry on across the body by themselves once started, so this waits for the medic's hands to be free again before returning. Returns the type of what was used, or null if nothing we carry would help. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_cryo_airThe tube's own gas: what its occupant breathes, and what cools them.
/proc/sp_cryoxadone_unitsCryoxadone in a container, in units.
/proc/sp_cryo_has_medicineWhether the tube has medicine left to give.
/proc/sp_cryo_gas_readyWhether the loop behind a tube has gas in it and is cold enough for cryoxadone to work.
/proc/sp_cryo_readyWhether a tube could take a patient right now: working, empty, stocked, gassed and cold.
/proc/sp_medbay_cryo_cellsEvery cryo tube in medbay on this z-level.
/proc/sp_find_cryo_cellThe nearest tube ready for a patient, or null.
/proc/sp_load_into_cryoPuts a patient we are pulling into a cryo tube the way a doctor does it: open the tube, drag them onto it, shut it. The tube switches itself on once it has an occupant. Somebody out cold can be lifted in with TG's drag-and-drop instead, which is only allowed for the incapacitated. Returns how they went in, or null if they did not. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_carried_cryoxadoneA cryoxadone container we carry with enough left in it to be worth putting in a tube.
/proc/sp_find_loose_in_roomThe nearest loose item of a type in a room — on the floor or a table, not in anybody's hands or bags.
/proc/sp_find_cryo_canisterA canister in the cryo room sitting on its port but not connected: how both of MetaStation's start the shift.
/proc/sp_cryo_freezersThe freezers in the cryo room.
/proc/sp_next_cryo_taskThe next thing the cryo tubes need before they can take a patient, as list(task, target), or null when there is nothing we can do: they are ready, they are still cooling, or what they lack is not in the room. Gas comes first. On MetaStation the loop starts the shift empty, with both canisters sitting on their ports unconnected, and a tube with no gas throws its patient straight back out.
/proc/sp_do_cryo_jobDoes one cryo setup job with the tool and the clicks a doctor would use. TRUE if it took. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_announce_cryo_readyTells medbay the tubes are set up, once a round.
/proc/sp_is_surgeonWhether this medic may operate. Surgery is the doctors' and the CMO's.
/proc/sp_needs_surgeryWhether a patient has something only the table fixes properly: a dislocated or broken bone.
/proc/sp_only_needs_surgeryA broken or dislocated bone and nothing a medkit or a cryo tube would be wanted for.
/proc/sp_bone_wound_countHow many broken or dislocated bones a patient has.
/proc/sp_tool_kind_nameWhat to call a tool kind in a log line: a tool behaviour already reads as a word; an item type needs its name.
/proc/sp_tool_doesWhether a tool does this job: an item of a type, or anything with that tool behaviour.
/proc/sp_find_toolThe first thing we carry that does one of these jobs, in order of preference.
/proc/sp_surgery_needsThe tools each procedure on this patient needs, start to finish, as a list of lists of alternatives.
/proc/sp_surgery_feasibleWhether we carry what it takes to see this patient's operation through, so nobody is left cut open halfway.
/proc/sp_find_optableThe nearest operating table in medbay with nobody on it.
/proc/sp_tray_toolsThe tools on a surgery tray that do any of these jobs, one per job.
/proc/sp_missing_surgical_kitThe jobs in the surgical kit we have nothing for.
/proc/sp_find_surgery_trayThe nearest surgery tray in the theatres with something on it we are missing.
/proc/sp_next_operationThe next operation a patient on the table needs, as list(operation type, zone, tools that will do it, damage type to tend), or null when there is nothing left but to let them up. Worked out afresh each time from the limbs themselves — skin, vessels and bones are all recorded on the limb — so an operation that is interrupted picks up where it left off. Bones first, then serious damage tended through the chest, then closing up whatever is open.
/proc/sp_perform_operationPerforms one operation on the patient with a tool we carry. TG has the surgeon pick the operation off a radial menu whenever more than one fits the tool and the spot, and a mob with no client gets no menu at all, so this finds the operation we want among those on offer and starts it the way the menu would. Returns TRUE if it went ahead. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_expose_for_surgeryGets clothing out of the way of the knife the way a surgeon would: a jumpsuit is rolled down off the chest and arms, and anything else over the spot comes off. Returns what was moved as list(item, slot) pairs for sp_redress() to put back; a null slot means rolled down. Nothing hanging off the jumpsuit — ID, belt, pockets — is dropped with it.
/proc/sp_redressPuts back what sp_expose_for_surgery() moved: jumpsuits rolled back up, everything else back on.
/proc/sp_bring_alongsideSteps somebody we are pulling onto a free tile beside target that is still beside us, the way a player drags a patient into position. Returns TRUE once they are next to it. Sleeps.
/proc/sp_do_surgeryOperates on a patient we have brought to the table: lays them on it, gets clothes out of the way, works through sp_next_operation() until there is nothing left, then dresses them and lets them up. Returns how many operations were done. Sleeps.
