/proc/sp_botany_areasAreas the botanist works in.
/proc/sp_tray_jobWhat, if anything, this tray needs from us right now. Null when it is fine or we cannot help.
/proc/sp_botany_watering_canThe botanist's watering can. Pass TRUE to require that it still holds water.
/proc/sp_find_tray_jobThe nearest tray in view that wants attention, along with the job it wants. Returns a list(tray, job). preferred_species is a plant somebody has asked for, ignored trays picked too recently to pick again.
/proc/sp_pick_seedA seed to plant, picked at random from what the botanist is carrying.
/proc/sp_find_loose_produceProduce or seed packets lying loose on the floor near the botanist, nearest first. Harvesting drops produce at their feet and the seed extractor spits packets out beside it, so this is how both get collected.
/proc/sp_plant_asked_forThe plant somebody just asked botany for, or null. The line has to name botany and the plant: the crew talk about food all shift, so a request is addressed, where a passing mention of aloe is not.
/proc/sp_named_delivery_areaWhere a request says its delivery should go ("up to medbay"), as an area type, or null if it names nowhere.
/proc/sp_plant_requestedWhether a botanist is already working on a request for this plant.
/proc/sp_requested_produceProduce we are carrying that somebody asked for, if anybody has.
/proc/sp_request_itemsEverything we carry for the request in hand: what it cooks into, if we have made that, then the plant itself.
/proc/sp_request_needs_cookingThe raw produce we carry for a request that still wants cooking before it is handed over.
/proc/sp_microwave_usableWhether a microwave would take something and cook it right now.
/proc/sp_find_microwaveThe nearest microwave on our level that would cook something for us now. Not sight-limited: the kitchen's pair are a wall away from the hydroponics trays.
/proc/sp_find_request_tableA table in the area that asked for produce, to leave their delivery on.
/proc/sp_carried_produceHow much produce the botanist is carrying.
/proc/sp_find_kitchen_tableA table in the kitchen to leave produce on, or null if we cannot find one on our z-level.
/proc/sp_find_sample_tableA table in hydroponics to display sample produce on.
/proc/sp_find_water_sourceThe nearest thing we can refill a watering can from.
/proc/sp_carried_seed_typesSeed typepaths the botanist is carrying packets of.
/proc/sp_produce_needing_seedsProduce worth turning into seeds: something we are carrying whose species we have no packet of. This is how a botanist locks in a mutation they just grew, so it is worth a walk to the extractor.
/proc/sp_find_seed_extractorThe nearest seed extractor. Not sight-limited, for the same reason as the cargo console.
/proc/sp_find_seed_vendorThe nearest working MegaSeed Servitor. Not sight-limited.
/proc/sp_buy_seed_packetBuys one packet of a seed the botanist does not already carry, paid for out of their own wages. Returns the seed, or null. Botanists earn a paycheck at spawn, so a few packets a shift is affordable.
/proc/sp_is_mutation_candidateA tray worth dosing with mutagen: growing, not already unstable, and of a species that has somewhere to mutate to. This is the botanist deliberately trying to breed something new. Is this tray still a plant worth pushing towards a mutation?
/proc/sp_find_mutagenAny cup, bottle or beaker we carry that still holds mutagen.
/proc/sp_cook_in_microwavePuts produce in a microwave the way a player does, one item in hand at a time, starts it, waits out the cook and takes whatever cooked_type comes out. Returns what was taken. Sleeps for the whole cook, ten seconds or so on a microwave nobody has upgraded.
/proc/sp_table_besideA table within a couple of tiles of spot and in the same room, to leave a delivery on rather than the floor.
/proc/sp_leave_forTells a crew member something was left for them, so they go and fetch it from wherever it had to be put down.
