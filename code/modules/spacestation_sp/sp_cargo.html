code/modules/spacestation_sp/sp_cargo.dm

/datum/sp_supply_requestCargo.
/proc/sp_request_suppliesAsks cargo for a crate. This is the entry point for every other department.
/proc/sp_supplies_on_orderWhether cargo already has this pack on the way: asked for, and not yet delivered.
/proc/sp_pending_requestsRequests cargo has not ordered yet.
/proc/sp_cargo_accountThe cargo budget.
/proc/sp_place_orderPuts a pack on the order list, paid from the cargo budget, exactly as the console would. Returns the order, or null if cargo cannot afford it.
/proc/sp_supply_docked_homeIs the supply shuttle sitting at the station?
/proc/sp_supply_awayIs the supply shuttle away at the depot?
/proc/sp_supply_movableCan we order the shuttle around right now?
/proc/sp_find_cargo_consoleThe nearest working supply console. Deliberately not an oview() search: oview is sight-limited, so a console one room away behind a wall is invisible and the quartermaster would never find their own desk.
/proc/sp_supply_shuttle_turfsEvery turf the supply shuttle occupies, so we can find what has just been delivered.
/proc/sp_find_crate_on_shuttleA crate still sitting on the supply shuttle that nobody else has hold of. Two technicians grabbing at the same crate just steal the pull off each other and neither finishes, so anything somebody already has hold of, or is on their way over to, is left to them.
/proc/sp_crate_claimedWhether somebody other than asking already has this crate: dragging it, or on their way over to. Checking only who was dragging left a gap the length of the walk to the shuttle, and two technicians who picked the same crate inside it took the pull off each other all the way across the station until both gave up.
/proc/sp_find_cargo_dropoffSomewhere in the cargo bay to leave a crate that nobody asked for.
/proc/sp_find_delivery_spotA spot inside area_type to leave a delivery. Picks the closest clear tile rather than a random one, so a technician dragging a crate across the station stops at the door rather than walking it into the far corner of the department.
/proc/sp_request_for_crateThe request a delivered crate belongs to. The supply shuttle ends every ordered crate's name with " - #", and the quartermaster keeps that number on the request, so the match is exact. Matching on the pack's name was not: a pack and its crate are often called different things, and the Medical Supplies Crate medbay asked for arrived as a "DeForest Medical crate", matched nothing, and was left in the cargo bay as if nobody wanted it.
/proc/sp_delivered_cratesCrates cargo has brought over for this pack and handed off, wherever they ended up.
/proc/sp_good_drop_spotSomewhere a delivery can sit without being in the way: open floor with room to walk round it, and neither in a doorway, where it jams the door, nor right in front of one, where everybody has to squeeze past it.
/proc/sp_reachable_drop_spotWhere somebody carrying a delivery can actually leave it, on the way to destination. Sleeps: it asks the pathfinder, and the pathfinder makes you wait.
