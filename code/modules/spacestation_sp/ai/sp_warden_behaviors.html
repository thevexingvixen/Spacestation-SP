code/modules/spacestation_sp/ai/sp_warden_behaviors.dm

/proc/sp_in_brigWhether something is in security's own rooms.
/proc/sp_note_prisonerNotes who was put in which cell and until when, for the warden.
/proc/sp_prisoner_entryThe entry for whoever is, or was, in this cell.
/proc/sp_in_cellWhether somebody is inside a cell: past its door on the inside, and near it.
/proc/sp_cell_openWhether any of a cell's doors stands open.
/proc/sp_cell_occupiedWhether anybody alive is in the cell.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_warden_check_cellsLooks over the cells. An escape is called in on the spot. A sentence that has run its course, or an empty cell left standing open, is a walk over (BB_SP_CELL_DUTY), which the rest of the sequence makes and sp_warden_cell_duty ends.
/proc/sp_warden_call_escapeA prisoner out of their cell before their time: put on the arrest list, called in, and the cell freed up.
/proc/sp_clear_cell_dutyLets go of a cell duty, done or not.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_warden_cell_dutyAt the cell: a release is a word and the record cleared, and a reset shuts the door again. A cell door does not shut itself: TG's timer opens it when the sentence ends and nothing closes it after, so a used cell stood open for the rest of the shift.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_warden_find_open_armouryAn armoury locker standing unlocked on a shift that is not red: something to lock up again. Their own belt comes out of one at the start of the shift (kit_wanted()), and this is what shuts it behind them.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_warden_secure_lockerShutting and locking it. Async, for the clicks.
