code/modules/spacestation_sp/ai/sp_favour_behaviors.dm

/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_crew_favourFavours: standing spent on things the crew do for you.
/proc/sp_favour_possibleWhether controller could do this favour for player just now: the thing a dialogue checks before offering it (the can_favour condition). What a door or a fetch turned out to be is handed back through found, so the dialogue can name it and the favour can go straight to it.
/proc/sp_favour_doorA shut airlock near player that crew can open and they cannot, and that crew will open for somebody else.
/proc/sp_door_off_limitsWhether a door is one nobody opens for somebody else: it needs an access on the list above.
/proc/sp_favour_fetchableSomething this crew member would fetch: one they carry and would hand straight over, or the nearest one lying about in their own department -- loose on a floor or a table, not in anybody's hands or shut in a locker. Null when there is nothing to be had.
/proc/sp_medic_to_callSomebody who would come if called: an AI medic, not a chemist, with the kit to see a patient.
/proc/sp_start_favourTakes a favour on. Returns TRUE if it was taken.
/proc/sp_end_favourGives a favour up or calls it done, with a word to them if there is one to say.
/proc/sp_favour_nameWhat we call the person we are doing a favour for (sp_what_we_call()).
/proc/sp_releases_favourWhether somebody is letting us off: thanks, or "that's all".
/proc/sp_favour_walkWalks towards target until within distance of it. Returns TRUE once there, FALSE while on the way, and null for a walk that has stopped getting any closer for SP_FAVOUR_STUCK -- a locked door in the way, somebody who keeps walking off -- which the favour then gives up.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_do_favourDoing whatever favour is in hand, a step at a time. Ends it when it is done, given up, or overtaken by work.
/proc/sp_favour_followComing with them: staying a step or two behind wherever they go, until let off, lost, or out of time.
/proc/sp_favour_hold_doorOpening a door for them: walk to their side of it, open it with our own ID -- nothing is opened that the card on our chest would not open -- and keep it open until they have gone through.
/proc/sp_past_doorWhether who is on the far side of a door from somebody standing at it.
/proc/sp_favour_fetchFetching something: go and get it -- unless it is already in our pockets -- then bring it back and hand it over.
/proc/sp_favour_call_doctorCalling medbay for them: a word on the radio, carried to the medics the way an incident is to security.
