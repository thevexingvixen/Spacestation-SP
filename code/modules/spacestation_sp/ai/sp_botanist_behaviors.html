/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

code/modules/spacestation_sp/ai/sp_botanist_behaviors.dm

/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_botanist_tendTend the nearest tray that wants something: harvest, clear, weed, water or plant.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_botanist_gatherPick up produce we have knocked loose on the floor.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_botanist_deliverCarry a full satchel of produce to the kitchen, leaving a sample out on the way.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_botanist_refillRefill the watering can when it runs dry.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_trayFinds the nearest tray that needs work and records what it needs.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_equip_for_trayPuts the right thing in our hands for the tray job we picked: nothing for harvesting and clearing, the hoe for weeds, the watering can for a dry tray, a random seed packet for an empty one.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_work_trayWorks the tray with whatever is in our hands, then forgets it so the next tick re-evaluates.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_produceSpots produce lying on the floor (harvesting drops it at our feet).
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_stow_producePicks the produce up and stows it, so our hands stay free for the next tray.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_has_deliveryTrue once we are carrying enough produce to be worth a trip to the kitchen.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_produce_dropFinds where this load goes: whoever asked for produce, when we are carrying what they asked for, and a kitchen table otherwise. Async for a request, since how close to them we can get is the pathfinder's to say: the second aloe of a round went to a medic standing in an operating room, behind doors no botanist opens.
/proc/sp_aim_at_kitchenPoints the delivery at the nearest kitchen table. Returns behaviour flags, to hand straight back out of a leaf.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_kitchenFinds the kitchen table we are going to unload onto.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_unload_produceUnloads the produce onto the table we are standing next to and tells the kitchen it is there.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_unload_produce/sampleSamples left out in hydroponics for whoever wanders past.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_sample_tableFinds a table in hydroponics to leave samples on.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_needs_water_refillTrue when we carry a watering can that has run dry and there is a tray that wants water.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_waterFinds something to refill the can from. A bucket, the office cart or any sink: all of them fill a mop.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_refill_canFills the can from the tank we are standing at.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_botanist_extractCarry produce of a species we have no seeds for to the extractor and turn it into packets.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_botanist_shopBuy a seed variety we do not own from the MegaSeed Servitor.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_botanist_experimentDose a growing plant with mutagen and see what it turns into.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_extractionFinds produce worth seeding and an extractor to do it in.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_run_extractorFeeds the produce into the extractor; the seeds drop out beside it for us to pick up.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_seed_vendorFinds a seed vendor worth visiting.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_buy_seedsBuys one packet of something we have never grown, out of our own wages.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_experimentFinds a growing plant that could mutate into something else, and mutagen to push it along.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_apply_mutagenPours mutagen into the tray. That raises the plant's instability, and an unstable plant has a chance every growth cycle to mutate into another species from its own mutation list.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_request_needs_cookingTrue when we carry raw produce for a request that ought to be cooked before it is handed over.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_microwaveFinds a microwave for requested produce, and a tile beside it to stand on.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_microwave_requestStanding at the microwave: cooks what was asked for and takes out what it makes. Async, for the whole cook.
