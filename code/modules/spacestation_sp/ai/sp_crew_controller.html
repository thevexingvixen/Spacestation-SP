/datum/ai_controller/sp_crewBase AI controller for Spacestation SP crew members (real /mob/living/carbon/human pawns).
/proc/sp_animal_in_the_wayAn animal that is only in the way: one TG will not swap places with (it is in combat mode, as basic mobs are), not fixed in place, and not after anybody. The HoS's giant spider sits beside the desk, in the one gap between the tables and the security consoles, and the pathfinder, which ignores mobs, plans straight through it; the HoS bumped into their own pet until every walk gave up, all shift. People step round a pet that will not budge.
/proc/sp_step_pastSwaps places with an animal in the way, the way mobs swap places when neither minds.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/medicalMedical staff: treat injured crew they can see, otherwise behave like base crew inside medical.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/medical/chemistChemists: brew medicine for medbay and keep the cryo tubes in cryoxadone; otherwise medical staff like the rest.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/assistantAssistants: no department, no work, and the whole shift to fill. Everything the rest of the crew do, plus a trip to tool storage for gloves and a tool, lockers left hanging open, cheekier grumbling at locked doors, and a little harmless mischief now and then (sp_greytide.dm). Greytide, gently.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/securitySecurity: patrol hallways and the brig, respond to reports, subdue and cuff attackers.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/engineerEngineering: keep the engine running and the SMES charged; otherwise wander engineering.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/botanistBotanist: runs the hydroponics trays and keeps the kitchen supplied.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/cargoCargo technician: gets the crates off the shuttle and walks the requested ones where they belong.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/cargo/quartermasterQuartermaster: the same, plus the paperwork and the shuttle itself.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/chefChef: stocks the prep table, works the cooking tree, and puts what comes out on the counter.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/bartenderBartender: mixes drinks at the taps and lines them up on the bar.
