/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_chemist_workChemist: pick what medbay needs, walk to a bench, and brew it — into patches, or a beaker for the cryo tubes.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_chemist_deliverChemist: take finished medicine where it is wanted — patches into the chemistry fridge, cryoxadone to cryo.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_pick_chem_orderPicks the next thing to make (sp_next_chem_order) and the bench to make it at.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_brew_orderStanding at the bench: brews the order (sp_make_medicine), printing it into patches if that is its form.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_has_chem_deliveryPasses while we carry finished medicine with somewhere to go.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_pick_chem_dropPicks where the medicine we carry goes into BB_SP_CHEM_DROP. It also sets a short wait before the next try, which a delivery that lands clears again: a fridge that cannot be reached is not walked at every tick, but a successful run goes straight on to the next, rather than sitting out a pause while the order board sends the chemist back to the bench.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_drop_off_chemsDrops the medicine off: every patch we carry into the chemistry fridge, or the cryoxadone onto the table in the cryo room, where the medics' own cryo setup will find it when a tube runs low. Says so on medical.
