code/modules/spacestation_sp/ai/sp_janitor_behaviors.dm

/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_janitor_suppliesFetch a mop, and water to put in it.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_janitor_cleanPick up the litter, then mop the nearest mess.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/janitorJanitor: fetches a mop, keeps it wet, and works through the mess.
/proc/sp_carried_mopThe mop we are carrying, wherever it is stowed.
/proc/sp_mop_is_wetWhether a mop has anything in it. mop.dm refuses to clean below 0.1 and tells the mopper it is dry.
/proc/sp_holds_waterWhether there is anything in a sink, bucket or cart to fill a mop from.
/proc/sp_mess_priorityHow much a mess wants cleaning: how far off it is, with maintenance pushed to the back of the queue. A janitor who simply took the nearest would spend the shift in the tunnels, where nobody sees the floor.
/proc/sp_janitor_noteSays, once a minute at most, why there is nothing being cleaned.
/proc/sp_litter_typecacheLitter that is picked up by hand rather than mopped. A peel first of all: people slip on those.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_needs_suppliesTrue when we have no mop, or the one we have is dry.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_mopFinds a mop lying about. Mops put themselves in GLOB.janitor_devices, so there is no search to do.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_take_mopPicks the mop up.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_wet_mopFills the mop. TG does the filling; this is the walk up to it and the check that it worked.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_messThe nearest mess worth walking to, maintenance last, skipping any we have already failed to reach.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_mop_messMops it. The cleaning is TG's own: the mop's cleaner component runs a do_after and the decal goes.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_litterA banana peel or a wrapper on the floor nearby.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_take_litterBins it. A peel in a bag is one nobody slips on, which is the whole point of picking it up.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_mess_unreachableThe mess could not be got to: remember the room rather than the stain.
