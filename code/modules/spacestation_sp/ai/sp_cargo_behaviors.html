/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_shuttle_needs_movingTrue when the shuttle should be moved: orders waiting at home, or sitting idle at the depot.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_run_supply_shuttleSends the shuttle out with the orders, or calls it back in with the goods.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_crateFinds a crate on the shuttle and works out where it belongs. Async for a crate somebody asked for: how close to them a technician can actually get is a question for the pathfinder, and the pathfinder makes you wait.
/proc/sp_start_haulSets a claimed crate off towards destination, or lets it go again if there is nowhere to take it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_grab_crateTakes hold of the crate, the way a technician drags one around.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_drop_crateLets go once the crate is where it belongs.
