code/modules/spacestation_sp/ai/sp_medical_behaviors.dm

/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_crew_patientA medic is working on us, or we are lying in a cryo tube or on the operating table: hold still.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_crew_checkupNothing better to do and carrying an injury: walk to medbay and wait to be seen.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_medical_patientMedical: see to the worst-off patient — walk over, look them over, then patch them up or take them to cryo.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_medical_cryo_setupMedical: get the cryo tubes ready — gas on, freezer cold, a beaker in every tube.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_medical_surgical_kitSurgeons: fetch the tools a doctor's medkit does not have from a surgery tray in the theatre.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_under_carePasses while we are being treated. TG's escape_captivity subtree reads a cryo tube as a box to break out of and an operating table as restraints, so without this an AI patient climbs straight back out of cryo. It also passes while a medic has asked us to hold still, so nobody wanders off mid-stitch.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_hold_stillStays put for the medic: faces them and does nothing else for a moment.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_wants_checkupHurt, not badly enough to drop everything, and somebody in medical is about to see to it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_await_treatmentWaits in medbay to be seen: succeeds once we are patched up, gives up after SP_CHECKUP_PATIENCE. This replaced a fixed fifteen-second wait, after which the safety subtree's cooldown sent the patient back towards their own department for twenty seconds before walking them to medbay all over again.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_patientPicks who to see next (sp_find_patient) into BB_SP_PATIENT. Fails when there is nobody.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_examine_patientLooks the patient over — with a health analyzer if we have one, which the patient sees us do — decides what to do with them (sp_triage), and tells them.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_treatment_isPasses when the medic has settled on this treatment for the patient.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_treat_patientWorks through the patient with whatever we carry, a round at a time (sp_treat_round), until there is nothing more we can do for them or they are done.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_bring_to_cryoTakes hold of the patient to walk them over to the cryo tube in BB_SP_CRYO_CELL.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_load_cryoAt the tube with the patient in tow: puts them in (sp_load_into_cryo).
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_cryo_needs_setupPasses while the cryo tubes still need something doing that we can do, and no other AI medic is on it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_plan_cryo_taskPicks the next cryo setup job (sp_next_cryo_task) into BB_SP_CRYO_TASK and BB_SP_CRYO_TARGET.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_do_cryo_taskDoes the planned cryo setup job (sp_do_cryo_job) once we are standing by it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_bring_to_tableTakes hold of the patient to walk them over to the operating table in BB_SP_OPTABLE.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_operateAt the table with the patient in tow: operates (sp_do_surgery).
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_needs_surgical_kitSurgeons short of a tool in GLOB.sp_surgical_kit, with a tray in the theatre that has it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_surgery_trayPicks the surgery tray to fetch tools from into BB_SP_SURGERY_TRAY.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_take_surgical_toolsTakes what we are missing off the tray, the way the tray's storage lets anyone take one thing at a time.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_medical_restockMedical: top up on treatment supplies, out of a locker, from cargo, or by asking botany for aloe.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_needs_restockTrue when a medic has too little left on them to treat anybody with.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_supply_storeFinds a locker or crate of supplies worth opening.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_take_suppliesStanding by it: opens it and takes what we need. Async, because a locked closet takes two clicks.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_ask_cargo_for_suppliesAsks cargo for a crate of medical supplies, at most every ten minutes.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_ask_botany_for_aloeAsks botany for aloe, at most every ten minutes: baked aloe is the cream medics treat burns with.
