/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

code/modules/spacestation_sp/ai/sp_body_behaviors.dm

/proc/sp_body_taken_byThe AI medic already seeing to this body, other than other_than, or null.
/proc/sp_drop_bodyForgets the body we were seeing to, done with or not.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_medical_bodySee to a body: go to it, try the paddles, and failing that the morgue. Above patients: a body has a window.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_bodyThe body we are seeing to, if it is still a body lying somewhere we might get to. Clears it otherwise.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_see_to_bodyBeside the body: the paddles, if TG says they would work and we have them; otherwise the morgue, with the body in tow. Succeeds only when there is a morgue trip to make. A body revived, or one nothing can be done for and nowhere to take, ends the visit here. Async.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/move_to_target/sp_reported/sp_haulingA walk with somebody in tow: no diagonal steps, so the pull survives corners (/datum/ai_movement/jps/sp_crew/escort).
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_store_bodyBeside the tray with the body: in it goes (sp_store_in_morgue). A pull lost on the way is taken again if the body is still in reach, and the trip starts over from the body if not. Async.
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/security/detectiveSecurity's investigator: an officer in every other respect, who goes to look at a body before anything else.
/proc/sp_drop_sceneForgets the scene.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_detective_sceneLook a scene over: go to the body, or to where it lay, and read it. Above the rest of security's work.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_sceneWhere to go: the body while it is lying somewhere, and once a medic has it on a tray, the tile it lay on, where whatever was dropped still is. Clears the scene when there is nothing left to look at.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_investigate_sceneAt the scene: read it (sp_investigate_scene). Async.
