/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

code/modules/spacestation_sp/ai/sp_crew_behaviors.dm

/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_crew_coreShared high-priority reactions: escape restraints, react to attackers, seek medbay, avoid armed people, answer to our name.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_department_wanderWalk to a random turf inside our department (or the areas in BB_SP_WANDER_AREAS), then linger.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_crew_safetyWhen hurt: shout for help and walk to medbay.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_crew_socialWhen someone addresses us by name: face them and answer.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_crew_defenseWhen attacked: report it (radio if we have one, otherwise shout) and get away.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_crew_threatWhen someone nearby is wielding a weapon: warn them and keep our distance.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_security_respondSecurity: respond to a reported incident, subdue and cuff the suspect.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_engineer_powerEngineering: when the station is short on power, go to the engine room and set the engine up.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_engineer_repairEngineering: walk to a hull breach and patch it with the RCD.
/datum/ai_movement/jps/sp_crewStation-wide pathfinding. TG's default AI movement caps paths at AI_MAX_PATH_LENGTH (30 tiles), which is fine for animals that lose interest after 14 tiles but leaves crew unable to walk to medbay, the engine room, or an incident on the far side of the station.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/move_to_target/sp_reportedA move_to_target that says so in the log when a walk fails. Inside a cooldown a failed walk is otherwise silent, and looks like somebody who set off and changed their mind: it took a whole round's log to spot a doctor who could never reach the theatre trays and a Head of Personnel stuck at a set of flaps.
/proc/sp_is_armedIs this human holding something we would call a weapon?
/proc/sp_armed_threatWhether somebody armed is a worry. Not security or command, who are allowed; and not a monkey with a knife until it starts swinging it. Pun Pun arms themselves with whatever turns up behind the bar, and crew in the bar spent whole minutes shouting for security about a monkey that was minding its own business.
/proc/sp_is_authoritySecurity and command are allowed to carry weapons without scaring the crew.
/proc/sp_message_is_distressWhether a line from a player is a call for help.
/proc/sp_is_shoutingWhether a line is written in capitals, the way people shout.
/proc/sp_free_handsPuts whatever is in our hands away, so the next step starts from a clean grip.
/proc/sp_reach_spotAn open tile to stand on with this in arm's reach, the nearest to near, or null if there is none; the thing's own tile counts when nothing there is in the way, as under a light switch. Walk there rather than to "within a tile" of the thing: the pathfinder will not end a walk on a diagonal whose two corners are both blocked, though an arm reaches across a table there. The theatre's surgery trays sit in corners with a table on one side and the operating table on the other, and the doctor sent to them never set off.
/proc/sp_ai_clickOne click, waiting out the click delays first.
/proc/sp_ai_drag_ontoOne drag-and-drop, the way a player drags a patient onto a cryo tube or an operating table.
/proc/sp_vend_productBuys one of something out of a vending machine, paid for from the buyer's own wages.
/proc/sp_crew_speakSpeaks for an AI crew member. If channel is given and the crew member wears a headset that has that channel (common is on every headset), the line goes over the radio; otherwise it is said out loud.
/proc/sp_equip_from_inventoryFinds the first item of one of types (in order of preference) anywhere in our inventory and puts it in our active hand. Returns the item, or null if we do not carry one or could not equip it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_pick_wander_turfPicks a random walkable turf inside one of a set of areas and stores it in target_key. Areas come from the blackboard list in areas_key, else from fixed_areas, else from the type of BB_SP_HOME_AREA (including subtypes).
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_pick_wander_turf/medbayWhere a patient goes to be seen. The lobby, if the map has one: the rest of medbay is behind doors only medical staff can open, and JPS will not path anyone through a door they have no access for, so a patient who picked a spot on the treatment floor never got there — they gave up and went home.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_pick_wander_turf/engineEngine room variant, used by the engineer power subtree.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_saySays one of lines, optionally facing the atom in face_key first. "%TARGET%" in a line is replaced with that atom's name, "%AREA%" with our current area. If radio_channel is set the line goes over the radio when we have a headset for it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_scan_threatsKeeps BB_SP_THREAT pointing at the nearest visible, conscious, armed non-authority human, or clears it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_report_attackReports the attacker in BB_SP_ATTACKER: over the radio if we have one, otherwise by shouting. Also records a structured incident in BB_SP_LAST_INCIDENT so security who hear us know where to go.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_expire_attackerForgets the attacker once they are gone or enough time has passed. Always succeeds.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_equip_itemPuts the first carried item of item_types into our active hand and stores it in target_key.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_equip_item/batonSecurity weapon: baton if we have one. Fails if we carry none (we then fight unarmed).
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_attack_targetMelee-attacks the mob in target_key with whatever we hold (combat mode on). One swing per perform.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_security_confrontHave a word with somebody a crime was reported against. Ranks below the response to actual violence.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_confront_suspectA word with somebody a crime was reported against, rather than a baton.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_lost_themThe suspect is somewhere we cannot walk to: say so on the radio, and hold on to the incident.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_give_up_suspectA suspect who cannot be reached at all: call it in, and after a while let it go.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_clear_incidentClears the current incident (and our own attacker memory). Always succeeds.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_power_checkSucceeds when the station needs an engineer at the engine (engine off or APCs draining).
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_run_engine_setupRuns the scripted coolant-loop setup (see sp_engineering.dm) and reports the result over the engineering channel.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_start_emittersStarts the emitters if the chamber is ready. Always succeeds (the engineer just reports otherwise).
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_target_incapacitatedPasses when the mob in key is down: not conscious, incapacitated, or restrained.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_target_securedPasses when the mob in key is already restrained or dead (nothing left for security to do).
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_breachFinds the nearest hull breach and a safe tile to patch it from. Fails when there is nothing to fix.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_announce_breachAnnounces the breach we are heading to over the engineering channel.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_wear_evaPuts on the EVA suit and helmet we carry, so vacuum work does not freeze us. Always succeeds.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_open_internalsPuts on a breath mask and opens an emergency tank before working next to vacuum. Always succeeds.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_close_internalsCloses internals once the work is done. Always succeeds.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_equip_item/rcdEquips the RCD every engineer carries.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_patch_breachLays plating over the breach we are standing next to.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_equip_item/sidearmSecurity sidearm: an energy gun if one has been drawn, else the disabler every officer starts the shift with.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_shoot_targetShoots the mob in target_key with whatever is in hand.
/proc/sp_casing_is_lethalA casing that stuns is not a lethal one. ammo_type holds instances once update_ammo_types() has run, but the gun's own Destroy() says it is "sometimes paths, sometimes atom", so both are handled.
/proc/sp_abandon_escortLets go of the prisoner and forgets the escort. Every way an escort can fail comes through here.
/proc/sp_swap_into_cellThe one move that puts a pulled prisoner in a cell and leaves the officer outside it.
/proc/sp_open_cell_doorsOpens a cell's doors for the handover and stops them shutting themselves again.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_jail_targetPutting a cuffed prisoner in the cell and starting the clock, with the officer ending up outside.
