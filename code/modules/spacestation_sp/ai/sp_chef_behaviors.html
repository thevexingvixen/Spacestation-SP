code/modules/spacestation_sp/ai/sp_chef_behaviors.dm

/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_chef_serveCarry a finished dish out to the counter.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_chef_cookCraft whatever the pile on the prep table currently supports.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_chef_collectTake cooked food back out of the oven and off the griddle.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_chef_prepCut, roll, grill, bake and grind raw stock into the parts recipes are made of.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_chef_mixMix dough and cheese in a bowl.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_chef_stockFetch ingredients out of the fridges and off the floor.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_chef_supplyAsk cargo for a food crate when the kitchen has nothing left.
/proc/sp_stash_on_tablePuts something we have just made back on the prep table, where the next step can find it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_prep_tableFinds the prep table and remembers it. Everything else in the kitchen hangs off this.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_has_dishTrue when we have something finished to put out and somewhere to put it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_take_dishTakes the dish in hand and works out which counter it is going on.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_plate_dishSets the dish down on the counter and says so.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_cook_dishCrafts one of the things the pile on the table currently allows.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_cookedSpots an oven or griddle with something finished sitting in it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_take_cookedEmpties the finished food out of the machine and switches it off behind us.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_prep_taskPicks the next rung of the cooking tree to climb, and where we have to stand to climb it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_hold_for_prepGets a grip on the job: the tool in hand for knife and rolling-pin work (the ingredient stays on the table, which is where processing requires it), the ingredient in hand for anything going in a machine.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_do_prepDoes the actual cutting, rolling, grilling, baking or grinding.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_mix_taskWorks out what is worth mixing, in what, and whether we need to stop at the sink on the way.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_work_mixOne leg of a mix. At the sink that means running water into the bowl; at the table it means pouring the dry goods in, at which point the reaction fires and drops dough or a cheese wheel at our feet.
/proc/sp_abandon_mixDrops the current mix so the next attempt starts fresh.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_stocking_neededTrue while there is stocking to do: an armful to put down, or a table with too little on it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_stockFinds the next fridge, cabinet or pile of food to raid.
/proc/sp_carried_ingredientsIngredients the chef is carrying that belong on the prep table.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_take_stockTakes an armful out of whatever we walked to, or unloads onto the table when that is where we are.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_kitchen_needs_suppliesTrue when the kitchen has been picked clean: the worktop is not stocked, we are not carrying an armful, and there is no fridge, cabinet or pile left in the kitchen with anything in it.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_ask_for_foodPuts a food crate on cargo's list and says so on the radio.
