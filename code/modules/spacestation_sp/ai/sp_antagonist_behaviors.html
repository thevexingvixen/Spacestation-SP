code/modules/spacestation_sp/ai/sp_antagonist_behaviors.dm

/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/antagonistA crew member with a hidden agenda. Any job could carry a scheme; this is the dedicated example that spends its shift on one, and it is what the admin verb spawns.
/datum/bt_node/subtree/sp_crew_schemePursue the scheme: find what to go for, get there, take it, lie low. Ranks below self-preservation.
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_has_active_schemePasses while there is a scheme still to finish.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_find_scheme_goalWorks out what the scheme wants us at right now. Latches completion first (so the shift we picked the item up we stop here and go back to looking innocent), then asks the scheme for its goal atom: the item, or the locker it sits in. Fails when there is nothing to go to.
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_take_scheme_itemStanding by the target: take it. Opens a locker it is shut in, pockets it, and gets caught if seen. Async.
/proc/sp_note_scheme_stuckSays so in the log when a scheme has nothing to go to, at most once every couple of minutes.
/proc/sp_reachable_itemThe nearest instance of item_type within reach: on us, on our tile or an adjacent one (an open locker's).
