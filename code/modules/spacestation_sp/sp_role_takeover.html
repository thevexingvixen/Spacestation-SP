/proc/sp_ai_crew_in_jobSpacestation SP — a player takes a role from the AI crew.
/proc/sp_free_slot_from_aiFrees one place in a job that AI crew hold, for a player joining as it. A dead holder just stops counting against the job and their body stays where it is; a living one goes off shift. Returns TRUE if a place was freed.
/proc/sp_send_off_shiftTakes a living AI crew member off the station for good: they say so, drop off the manifest, their bank account closes, and a moment later they are gone.
/proc/sp_finish_off_shiftThe end of going off shift: the crew member, and the mind TG would otherwise keep, leave the round.
