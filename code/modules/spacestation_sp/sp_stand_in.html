code/modules/spacestation_sp/sp_stand_in.dm

/datum/sp_stand_inThe stand-in player: a debug tool that pays the crew a short, scripted visit the way a player would, so the player's side of conversation can be checked without anybody at the keyboard.
/proc/sp_stand_in_hostSomebody to visit who is not in the middle of anything. Somebody whose job has something to fetch first, so the favours can all be asked for; somewhere public before anywhere else, where a player would bump into them -- two minutes in, most of the crew are at their posts.
/proc/sp_stand_in_spotA clear tile of station floor exactly distance from somebody, in their sight. Floor, and not out in space: in sight includes the far side of a window, and the second round of favours sent the stand-in out into the vacuum beside a hallway, where nobody heard it say hello and it came back hurt.
/proc/sp_stand_in_linksThe links in an offer, as list(parameters, label) pairs, the way a chat window lays them out.
