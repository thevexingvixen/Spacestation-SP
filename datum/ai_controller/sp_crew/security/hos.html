hos
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/security/hos

The Head of Security: the one member of the department who gives orders rather than only taking them.

Orders travel the way crime reports already do (see on_pre_hear): the issuer writes the structured order onto their own blackboard, then says it out loud on the security channel, and whoever hears them reads it off the speaker. Nothing is broadcast to a roster, so an officer out of radio contact genuinely misses the order, and an HoS with no headset gives none at all. That is what we want from a chain of command.
