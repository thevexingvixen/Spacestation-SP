medical
/datum/ai_controller/sp_crew/medical

Medical staff: treat injured crew they can see, otherwise behave like base crew inside medical.

Procs

busy_with_workA medic with a patient has their hands full. Only the patient counts: sp_find_patient clears it whenever there is nobody to see, where a cryo setup job can be left set by a walk that failed, which would keep the medic "busy" all shift. A setup job is a few quick clicks, and one started over does no harm.
equip_extra_gearA compact defibrillator in the bag. TG hands doctors none: the full unit sits in their locker and the compact one in the CMO's. A medic sent to a body without paddles can only take it to the morgue (sp_body.dm), so they carry one from the start, as the CMO does.
fetchablesMedbay storage keeps its medkits out on the tables, and the lobby its gauze and mesh.
on_heard_callMedbay asked to come to somebody: one medic goes, whoever hears it first and is free, and says so. They then come to that person wherever they are, as though they were in sight (sp_find_patient()).
on_heard_incidentA body called in: one medic goes, whoever is free and hears it first, the way a house call works.
on_sent_off_shiftGoing off shift with a patient: they are unstrapped from the table and no longer told to wait for us.
take_bodyTakes a body on, if free to and nobody else has. TRUE if taken.
watch_cryoKeeps an eye on a cryo tube we put somebody in. The tube lets them out by itself once they are mended (or dead, or the gas runs out), and this is how the round's tally hears about it.
