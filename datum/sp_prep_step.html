sp_prep_step
/datum/sp_prep_step

One rung of the cooking tree: an ingredient, what you do to it, and what you get.

/tg/ hangs these transformations off elements and components on the ingredients themselves, so they cannot be enumerated from the item. Rather than have the chef poke every ingredient with every tool to find out, the rungs worth climbing are written down here. result is only used to know when to stop: once max_result of them are on the table there is no sense making more.
