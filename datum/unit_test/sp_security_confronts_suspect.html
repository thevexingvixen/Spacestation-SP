sp_security_confronts_suspect
/datum/unit_test/sp_security_confronts_suspect

A crime reported with a suspect and no attacker sends security to have a word, not to swing a baton.

Security's existing response is for people who hit people. Routing a smashed light tube through it would have an officer beating an assistant senseless, which is the disproportion this branch exists to avoid.
