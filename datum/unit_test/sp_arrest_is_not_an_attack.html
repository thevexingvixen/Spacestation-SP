sp_arrest_is_not_an_attack
/datum/unit_test/sp_arrest_is_not_an_attack

A person being arrested shouting that the officer attacked them is not a crime report.

Without this one arrest becomes a brawl: seen in a live round, where the suspect radioed it in, every officer took the arresting officer for an attacker, and four of them fought each other in a hallway while the suspect walked away.
