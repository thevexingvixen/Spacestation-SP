sp_dialogue_thread
/datum/sp_dialogue_thread

A dialogue somebody is in the middle of.

Vars

castrole name -> mob.
currentThe node said next, or null when the thread is done.
eventThe station event a rumour is about, when one was needed to start (the recent_event condition).
lines_saidLines said so far, player replies included.
next_dueWhen the next line is due.
pending_optionsA player's turn: the replies on offer, in order, and when the offer lapses.
started_atWhen it began, so no conversation outlasts SP_DIALOGUE_MAX_TIME.

Procs

advanceSays the line this thread is up to and works out the next one, or offers a player their replies. Returns FALSE when the thread is over, which is also what a pair who have drifted apart comes to.
chooseA player picks a reply. Refused for anybody but the player in the thread, for an offer that has lapsed or been replaced, and for a player who has walked off. The reply is said aloud through their own character, so everybody nearby hears it the ordinary way. Returns TRUE if it was taken.
expireThe player said nothing in time: silence is an answer, and the file says where it goes.
offer_optionsA player's turn: the replies whose conditions hold, offered as links for SP_DIALOGUE_ANSWER_TIME.
show_optionsThe replies, as links in the player's chat. A click comes back through Topic().
