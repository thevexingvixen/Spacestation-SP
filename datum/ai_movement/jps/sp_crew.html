A move loop that ends by itself (the pawn held still by a do_after mid-step, which deletes it; a target that was deleted) leaves the controller marked as moving with no loop behind it, and TG then treats every walk after it as a change of target for a loop that is not there: nothing is created, nobody moves, and nothing fails.
