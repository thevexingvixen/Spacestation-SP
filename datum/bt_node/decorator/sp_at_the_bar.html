sp_at_the_bar
/datum/bt_node/decorator/sp_at_the_bar

True when we are standing in the bar with a bartender in earshot.

This is what gives the bar something to do with no players on. A bartender who only ever pours the house menu at nobody is scenery; one taking orders off the crew who wander in is a bar.
