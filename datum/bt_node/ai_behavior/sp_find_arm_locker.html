Finding somewhere an officer can actually draw a kit from.

Their own locker is ACCESS_BRIG, which every officer carries. The armoury is ACCESS_ARMORY, which only the head of security and the warden hold -- so this deliberately does not send anyone there. The locker has to hold a belt, checked here rather than discovered on arrival: walking across the station to a locker with nothing in it is the same wasted trip as picking a target nobody can reach.
