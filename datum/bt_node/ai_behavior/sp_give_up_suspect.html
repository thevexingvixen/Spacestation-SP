sp_give_up_suspect
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_give_up_suspect

A suspect who cannot be reached at all: call it in, and after a while let it go.

Security carry brig and maintenance access, so a suspect sitting in the Head of Personnel's office or in atmospherics is genuinely out of reach, and the pathfinder is right to refuse. An officer who keeps trying is an officer who never does anything else for the rest of the shift, so after SP_SUSPECT_PATIENCE the word is called in over the radio and the suspect is dropped. The record keeps the crimes either way.
