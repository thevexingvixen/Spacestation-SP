sp_jail_target
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_jail_target

Putting a cuffed prisoner in the cell and starting the clock, with the officer ending up outside.

The officer arrives standing just inside the door, which leaves the prisoner they are pulling just outside it, and the handover is one move back out (sp_swap_into_cell()). Ending up outside is not cosmetic: timer_start() shuts the linked doors itself, so an officer still inside would serve the sentence alongside them. A prisoner who breaks the pull on the way is not dragged back; they are simply still at large.
