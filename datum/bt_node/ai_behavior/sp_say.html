sp_say
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_say

Says one of lines, optionally facing the atom in face_key first. "%TARGET%" in a line is replaced with that atom's name, "%AREA%" with our current area. If radio_channel is set the line goes over the radio when we have a headset for it.

Vars

face_keyOptional blackboard key holding an atom to face / name in the line.
linesLines to pick from.
radio_channelOptional radio channel (RADIO_CHANNEL_*) to try first.
