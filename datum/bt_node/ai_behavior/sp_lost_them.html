sp_lost_them
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/sp_lost_them

The suspect is somewhere we cannot walk to: say so on the radio, and hold on to the incident.

Officers carry brig and maintenance access, not atmospherics or engineering, so a suspect who wanders into one is genuinely out of reach and the pathfinder is right to refuse. Giving up on them would be wrong -- they have to come out -- and silently retrying is what made an arrest that never happened look like an arrest that was never called. This is a note in passing rather than something an officer stands and does: it always fails, so the shift carries on and the incident stays open.
