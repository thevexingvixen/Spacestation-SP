Setting a held energy gun to match standing orders before firing it.

select_fire() cycles rather than sets -- select++, wrapping at ammo_type.len -- so reaching a particular mode means cycling until it comes round, bounded by the number of modes. A disabler has one casing and no lethal setting at all, so this does nothing whatsoever to the weapon an officer starts the shift with: going lethal really does mean drawing an energy gun from the armoury first.

Succeeds even when there is nothing to set, because the shot that follows is no worse for it.
