sp_kitchen_mix
/datum/sp_kitchen_mix

Something made by pouring reagents into a bowl. /tg/ handles these as chemical reactions rather than crafting recipes, so they are the one part of the tree the crafting code cannot reach — and without them the chef has no dough and no cheese, which means no bread, no buns and no sandwiches.

Vars
